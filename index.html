A little about me

I'm Sai, a computer science student at the University of Michigan (B.S.E., class of 2028). I like building software that real people use, and lately that has mostly meant working with LLMs: multi-agent systems, AI tutors, and the tests and tracing that keep them reliable.

This past summer I was an analytics intern at KCF Technologies. I dug into vibration-sensor data with SQL, PySpark and Databricks to cut a model's false positive/negative rate by about 7%, and trained a PyTorch autoencoder to spot equipment problems. Before that I taught Python and Java to K–12 students at iCode School, which taught me a lot about explaining things simply.

Away from the keyboard, I'm usually on a long run, playing chess or basketball, watching UFC or anime, or losing a few rounds of Marvel Rivals.

Tempo In progress

Invent a chess variant by writing its rules in a short text file, and Tempo turns it into a program that knows every legal move. It already handles King of the Hill, Antichess and Atomic, matches Fairy-Stockfish on every one, and is tested with a fuzzer that writes random rules files.
